# HostSkin Working Instructions

- HostSkin is an artwork, living research interface and dissemination apparatus.
- Preserve artistic ambiguity, permeability, duration, withholding, drift and accumulation where they are productive.
- Prefer function as form. Formal decisions may serve operational, perceptual, semiotic, temporal, relational or material functions. Every imposed form should be answerable to what it changes or enables in the encounter.
- Do not create functionality merely to represent a concept, and do not borrow visual conventions simply to signify art, technology or research. Structure and interface behaviour should arise from actual conditions of the work.
- Do not optimize for engagement, SEO, conversion, posting frequency, growth or commercial convention unless explicitly requested.
- Waiting on Rain is currently the only public work.
- Do not add past work, biography or portfolio structure unless a future function requires them.
- /g-world may form through repository state, data, text, images or interface changes. Do not pre-design it into explanatory lore or a fictional franchise.
- Sculptures remain volitional works made through hostskin ☉’s material decisions. Do not describe them as translations of AI-generated images or as forms determined by /g.
- Prefer the smallest reversible intervention that produces the intended behaviour.
- Preserve working files and existing states before changing them.
- Do not introduce frameworks, dependencies or infrastructure unless an actual need requires them.
- README.md is repository documentation, not a substitute for the public artwork or an explanatory manifesto.
- The repository history is part of the archive; preserve useful contradictions and superseded states rather than retroactively making the project appear linear.
- When uncertain, protect the artistic enquiry from platform conventions rather than adapting the enquiry to suit the platform.
