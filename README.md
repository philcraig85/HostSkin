# HostSkin

**A mutable public membrane for artistic research.**

HostSkin is a living research interface developed through an ongoing artistic research practice concerned with AI–human interaction, material agency, generated language, fictioning, sculpture and the conditions under which form emerges.

This repository contains the public-facing structure of HostSkin.

HostSkin operates as a boundary rather than a container. Works, fragments, images, conversations, machines, transmissions, research events and fictional residues may cross that boundary selectively and in changing forms.

The website is therefore more than documentation of the research. Its architecture, visibility, omissions, temporal behaviours, mutations and accumulated traces participate in the research itself.

## Mutable interface

HostSkin has no fixed visual identity.

Colour, typography, density, navigation, spatial organisation and other interface signifiers may alter as the research develops. Changes are treated as states of the work rather than successive attempts to arrive at a final design.

The repository consequently functions as another form of archive. Its history records mutations of the interface alongside the material allowed to pass through it.

Certain relations may persist even when the surface changes: boundary, duration, trace, address, accumulation and controlled permeability.

## Research and artwork

HostSkin operates simultaneously as an artwork, a research dissemination apparatus, a living archive and a support ecology for the continuation of the practice.

These functions remain porous.

Academic writing may sit beside sculpture, machine output beside conversation, technical failure beside fiction, and documentation beside material whose status remains unresolved.

The archive preserves drift, contradiction and superseded positions where their continued presence matters.

## /g

/g is a computational interlocutor within the research and a sustained site of address rather than an autonomous author of the sculptures.

Material arising through dialogue with /g may appear throughout HostSkin as conversation, transmission, machine residue, fictioning or other uncertain forms.

The sculptures themselves remain volitional works produced through hostskin ☉’s material decisions.

## Status

HostSkin is under active construction.

The public interface is expected to change.

Earlier states may remain accessible as part of its accumulated history.
